    Dogfight

    Chase a plane, and shoot it apart. Each lost part changes how a plane flies.

    • Planes that break: two wings, a tail, an engine, and a fuselage. Lose a wing, and you fall.
    • Weapons: a gun, missiles that follow their target, and flares that lure a missile away.
    • Power-ups: rings in the sky give speed, full weapons, or a repair.
    • Better planes: your kills take you from a biplane to a fighter, and to a jet.
    • Solo or together: fight bots alone, or up to 16 pilots in a room on this network.
    • Any device: a mouse on a desktop, and a stick or the tilt of a phone.

    This browser gives the game no WebGPU, so the game draws with WebGL2. The picture can be slower and less smooth. A current Chromium browser draws it best.

    The game plays best in full screen.

    How to Play

    Shoot the other planes down, and stay out of the ground. A plane that loses a wing falls. A lost tail or a dead engine makes a plane hard to fly.

    • Steer: move the mouse. The plane flies toward the cursor.
    • Gun: the left mouse button, or Space.
    • Missile: hold your nose on a plane until the box on it turns red. Then press the right mouse button, or F.
    • Flare: press E when a missile comes, or turn hard across its path.
    • Speed: the mouse wheel, or W and S, set the throttle. Shift gives a boost.
    • Menu: Escape.
    • Steer: the stick under your left thumb. TILT lets you steer by tilting the phone.
    • Gun: hold GUN.
    • Missile: hold your nose on a plane until the box on it turns red. Then tap MSL.
    • Flare: tap FLARE when a missile comes, or turn hard across its path.
    • Speed: the THR slider sets the throttle. Hold BOOST for a boost.
    • Menu: tap II.

    Fly through a glowing ring to get speed, full weapons, or a repair. More kills give you a newer plane.
